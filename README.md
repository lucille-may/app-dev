# My Favorite Series: The Mentalist

---

## About the Series

**The Mentalist** is an American *crime drama* that aired on CBS from **2008 to 2015**. It follows **Patrick Jane**, a former fake psychic who now uses his sharp observation skills to help the California Bureau of Investigation (CBI) solve murder cases.

> Jane doesn't use guns or force. He reads people, and that is what makes him dangerous to criminals.

## Main Characters

- **Patrick Jane** (Simon Baker) – the consultant with a talent for reading people
- **Teresa Lisbon** (Robin Tunney) – the CBI team leader
- **Kimball Cho** (Tim Kang) – the calm and serious agent
- **Wayne Rigsby** (Owain Yeoman) – the loyal and funny agent
- **Grace Van Pelt** (Amanda Righetti) – the smart and dependable agent

## Why I Like It

1. The cases are clever and keep me guessing.
2. Jane's humor and tricks are entertaining.
3. The team has great chemistry.
4. The mystery of **Red John** keeps the story exciting across the seasons.
